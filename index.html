Privacy Policy – Stotra – Anugraha

Effective date: 19 October 2026
Developer: Anugraha Apps (Vignesh R)

Overview

Stotra – Anugraha is a free, offline devotional app for reading Hindu stotras. It does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not sell your data.

Information we collect

The app uses Google Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage statistics so we can improve the app. This includes:

  • App interactions (screens opened, features used, stotras read)
  • Device information (device model, OS version, language, country/region)
  • Anonymous device identifiers used by Firebase (including the Android Advertising ID, used only for analytics)

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or any personal information. Bookmarks, favourites and settings are stored only on your device.

How we use it

Analytics data is used solely to understand which features and stotras are used, fix bugs, and prioritise improvements. It is not used for advertising or profiling.

Third parties

Analytics data is processed by Google Firebase under Google's Privacy Policy. We do not share data with any other third party.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can reset or opt out of the Advertising ID in your device's Google settings. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored data.
